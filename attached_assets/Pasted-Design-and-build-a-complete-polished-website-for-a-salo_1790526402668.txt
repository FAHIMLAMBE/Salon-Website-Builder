Design and build a complete, polished website for a salon called **Nebula**. Make it responsive, fast, accessible, and ready to run. Use your judgment to create a distinctive visual identity rather than a generic salon template.

## Brand and visual direction
Create a modern, premium salon feel with a subtle cosmic touch inspired by the name “Nebula.” Use a refined palette such as deep plum or midnight violet, warm ivory, muted rose, and restrained champagne-gold accents. Keep the result elegant and welcoming, not overly dark or glittery.

Choose stylish, readable fonts that suit a beauty brand—perhaps an expressive serif for headings paired with a clean sans-serif for body text. Use generous spacing, strong typography, high-quality salon imagery, and carefully composed layouts. Add tasteful motion: gentle fades and slide-ins on scroll, smooth anchor scrolling, refined hover states, and subtle transitions. Keep animations lightweight, respect reduced-motion preferences, and avoid distracting effects.
